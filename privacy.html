Privacy Policy

BixTech internship portal, Cameroon. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and password, the field, course and duration you choose, and the school internship application form you upload. If you sign in with Google, Apple, GitHub, Facebook or Microsoft, we receive only your name and email address from that provider. While you are an intern, tutors record your daily attendance (present or absent).

How we use it

We use this information only to run the internship: to create and secure your account, review your application, show your status, and count your attendance. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Passwords and security

Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are stored as a salted hash. Sign-in uses a secure cookie. Only BixTech tutors and administrators can see applications, uploaded forms and attendance.

Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it. Our hosting providers store the data on our behalf.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data at any time by writing to bixtech3@gmail.com.
